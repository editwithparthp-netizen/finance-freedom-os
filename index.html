8-year roadmap

Year 1 — Foundations

Personal finance, market basics, risk, spreadsheets, writing and consistent paper-trading records.

Year 2 — Skills & proof

Build a portfolio of educational articles, calculators, journals and explainers. Review paper-trading data honestly.

Year 3 — Audience

Publish useful beginner-friendly content. Learn editing, research, communication and customer feedback.

Year 4 — First product

Test a free or low-cost learning tool. Keep content educational and transparent.

Year 5 — Business systems

Improve product quality, support, bookkeeping and legal understanding.

Year 6 — Sustainable growth

Build repeatable distribution and partnerships; avoid promises of profits.

Year 7 — Diversification

Expand educational products and responsible long-term saving habits.

Year 8 — Review & independence

Measure income, expenses, savings and business resilience. Adapt targets to real results.
